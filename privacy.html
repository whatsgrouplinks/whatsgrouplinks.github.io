Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect minimal information: when you submit a group, we store the group name, invite link, category, country, and description you provide. When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email, and message. We do not require accounts and do not track you across the web.

2. How we use it

Submitted group data is used solely to display the listing in the directory. Contact messages are used only to respond to your inquiry.

3. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary technical cookies (if any). We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies ourselves. Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense, when enabled) may use cookies to serve personalized ads; you can opt out via your browser settings or Google's ad settings.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Group listings you submit are public by design. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Your rights (GDPR/CCPA)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, including removal of a group you submitted, by contacting us. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children

This directory is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is always posted on this page.
