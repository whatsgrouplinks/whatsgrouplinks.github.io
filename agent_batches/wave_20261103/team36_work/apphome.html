The Right Way to Join Any Public WhatsApp Group

Some people may not know how to join a group. Don’t worry, we will guide you through the process step by step. Just follow the simple instructions below.

  1. Find the category of the group you want to join.
  2. Once ready, select one of the groups in that category.
  3. After selecting the group, click on the “Join In” link.
  4. You will be redirected to WhatsApp, and a pop-up will appear.
  5. In the pop-up, you will see the group name and a Join chat button.

Top FAQs About WhatsApp Group Links

Q.1 How to join a WhatsApp group without admin permission?

You cannot join any WhatsApp group without permission from the admin. If you want to join the group, you must request the admin.

But if you have a group invitation link, then you can easily join the group through that link without admin permission.

Q.2 How to create & find WhatsApp group links?

You won’t need to create a group link. When you create a group, the WhatsApp group invite link is generated automatically.

Now let’s see how you find the invitation link very quickly. Follow the steps below to get the group link.

  1. First, go to WhatsApp.
  2. Then go to the group you are the admin of.
  3. Once in the group, click on the three dots at the top right.
  4. Scroll down. You will see the option Invite via the link. Click on it and copy the link.

You can share this link with your friends, family members, etc. Otherwise, you can make the link public on other platforms. People can join your group from that platform.

Q.3 How to rejoin the WhatsApp group?

You have been removed from the WhatsApp group, and you want to rejoin the group. So, first of all, you need to read all the group rules.

After you are ready, request the group admin to add you back to the group, or you can also ask them for a group invitation link. So you can use that invitation link to rejoin the WhatsApp group.

Q.4 How to share WhatsApp group links?

The procedure is straightforward if you want to share a WhatsApp group link. Follow the steps below.

  1. Go to your WhatsApp.
  2. Select your group.
  3. Now go to the three dots, i.e., more options.
  4. Go to the Group Info option.
  5. Click on Invite Via Link
  6. There will be many options such as sending a link through another app, copying a link, sharing a link Via WhatsApp, and an Author.
  7. Select the option you like and share the link with your loved ones.

If you have any problems after following the steps, you can check it on its official website.

Q.5 How to Leave & Delete the WhatsApp Group?

Follow this step if you want to leave the group or delete the group.

  1. Open your WhatsApp.
  2. Go to the group you want to leave.
  3. Now go into “More Options”.
  4. Now choose the “Group Info” option.
  5. Scroll down a bit. There, you will be given the option to Exit the Group.
  6. After exiting, the delete group option will appear in the same place. Just click on it and delete the group.

Now you have successfully left the WhatsApp group and also deleted it.

Q.6 Is it safe to join a WhatsApp group with the link?

Yes, joining a WhatsApp group via an invitation link is 100% safe.
