Tips for Using Public WhatsApp Groups Safely

Public WhatsApp groups may have people you don’t know, so use the same caution you would in any online community.

Keep these simple rules in mind:

  • Do not share passwords, OTP codes, or banking details.
  • Avoid posting private documents in public groups.
  • Do not send money to unknown people without checking who they are.
  • Leave any group that contains suspicious or unwanted content.
  • Do not click on unknown files or suspicious external links.
  • Respect other members and follow the rules set by group admins.
  • Block and report users who send spam or inappropriate messages.
  • Parents and guardians should supervise younger users when needed.
