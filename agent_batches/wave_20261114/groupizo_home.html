The Origin

"I got tired of clicking WhatsApp invite links that led nowhere — dead pages, expired invites, groups at full capacity. The directories I found weren't maintaining their listings, so I started building Groupizo as a side project in early 2026. The idea is straightforward: every group gets reviewed before it goes live. After that, our system monitors invite status and updates listings when changes are detected. If a link stops working, the listing is flagged. We only look at what the admin provides — name, image, description. We do not enter groups or read any messages."

🧑‍💻
Elijah, Founder

How Groupizo works

Here's how a group gets from someone's phone to this page and stays here only if the link keeps working.

🔍

1. Someone submits their group

They paste their WhatsApp invite link, pick a category and country, and add a short description. It goes into a review queue, nothing goes live automatically if score is less than 80.

🤖

2. We check it and publish

The link gets reviewed manually. If it's active, the category fits, and it's not spam, it goes live. After that, our system re-checks every invite link on its own. Broken or revoked links get flagged and removed.

✅

3. You find and join

Search by topic, city, or country. Tap a group card, then tap "Join Group." WhatsApp opens straight to the join screen. No account on Groupizo needed ever.

Common questions

Questions people usually ask about Groupizo and WhatsApp groups. Something else on your mind? Send us a message.

How do I join a WhatsApp group from Groupizo?

Find a group you like, tap 'Join Group' on its page, and WhatsApp will open right at the join screen. That's it. You don't need to sign up for anything on Groupizo.

Are the group links on this site checked?

Yes. Every group goes through a manual review before it gets listed — we check that the invite link actually opens, that the name and category make sense, and that the submission looks genuine. After it's published, our system keeps an eye on the invite status and updates the listing if something changes. If a link stops working, we either remove it or mark it as inactive.

Why does a link say it's been revoked?

WhatsApp lets group admins reset their invite link whenever they want. When that happens, the old link stops working for everyone. Groups also stop accepting new members once they hit 1,024 people. Our checker catches most broken links within a day, but if you spot one first, use the Report button on the group page.

What does Groupizo actually check?

We check that the invite link is live and that the group exists with the name and image the person submitted. We don't read or monitor conversations inside any WhatsApp group — what goes on in there is between you and that community.

How do I add my own group?

Go to the Submit page, paste your WhatsApp invite link, fill in the category and location, and send it in. If the link works and the group fits our guidelines, it usually goes live within 24 hours. No charge, no account needed.

How do I leave a WhatsApp group quietly?

Open the group chat, tap the group name at the top, scroll down, and select 'Exit Group.' Newer versions of WhatsApp let you leave without sending a notification to the group. If you'd rather stay but stop the noise, just mute the group instead.

How do I stop spam after resetting my group link?

Go to group info, tap 'Invite via link,' and hit 'Reset link.' The old link stops working right away, and only people with the new one can join. You can also go into the group's admin settings and restrict who's allowed to add new members.

Does Groupizo cost anything?

No. Browsing groups, searching, joining, and submitting your own group are all free. There's no paid plan, no login, and no catch.

How often are new groups added?

New groups come in through people submitting them. Most get reviewed and published within 24 hours. How many show up each day depends on the category and region, but there's a steady flow.
