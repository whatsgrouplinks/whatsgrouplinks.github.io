  • Overseas and Gulf country jobs
  • Part-time and student jobs
  • Industry-specific roles like IT, healthcare, engineering, poetry groups finance, teaching, etc.

Once you join, you can receive daily job alerts, application deadlines, salary updates, interview tips, and direct HR contact details, depending on the group’s rules.

Important Safety Tips Before Joining

Even though these groups are useful, you must stay safe and avoid scams. Here are essential tips:

✔ Never share personal or sensitive information

Avoid sending your CNIC, passport, banking data, or personal documents in any group.

✔ Avoid paying for job offers

Genuine jobs do not ask for payment or “registration fees”.

✔ Verify the company’s name before applying

Always cross-check on Google or LinkedIn.

✔ Leave any group that shares spam or inappropriate content

Good groups maintain strict moderation and verified postings.

✔ Contact HR only on official numbers

Avoid unknown links or private inbox conversations unless fully verified.
