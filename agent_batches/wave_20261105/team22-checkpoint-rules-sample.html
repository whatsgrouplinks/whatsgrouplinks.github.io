WhatsApp Group Rules

Follow these simple rules so everyone has a good experience in the group.

  • Stay on topic. Do not post messages that are not related to the group.
  • Do not spam. Avoid forwarded messages, chain messages, and unrelated links.
  • Be respectful. No insults, no bullying, no personal attacks on other members.
  • Respect privacy. Do not share anyone's photo or personal information without permission.
  • Write clearly. Check your message before sending so it is easy to understand.
  • Keep private talks private. Move one-on-one conversations to a direct message.
  • Ask before adding members. Do not add people to the group without their permission.
  • Avoid sensitive topics. Do not discuss politics or religion unless the group is made for that purpose.
  • Leave politely. A short message before you leave is better than leaving without notice.
  • Mute instead of complaining. If the group is too active for you, use the mute option.
  • Close the group when it is no longer needed. If the group has served its purpose, you may leave or delete it.

Following these simple rules helps keep the group friendly and useful for all members.

How to Join This WhatsApp Group

You can join a WhatsApp group easily from a phone or a computer.

On Mobile (Android and iPhone):

  1. Tap the group invite link.
  2. WhatsApp will open and show a preview of the group, including its name, photo, and number of members.
  3. Tap Join Group to become a member.

If the group uses a QR code instead of a link, iPhone users can scan it with the camera app. Android users may need a QR code scanner app from the Play Store.

On Computer:

  1. Click the invite link on your computer.
  2. This will usually open WhatsApp Web in your browser.
  3. Click Join Group to confirm.

How to Leave a WhatsApp Group Without Notifying Others

You can leave a WhatsApp group quietly. Only the group admin will see that you left — other members will not get a notification.

Steps to leave quietly:

  1. Open the group chat.
  2. Tap the group name to open group information.
  3. Scroll down and tap Exit Group.
  4. Tap Exit again to confirm.

Other members will not receive an alert. However, they can still see that you left if they check the member list later.

How to Delete a WhatsApp Group

Only a group admin can delete a group completely. Even as an admin, you cannot delete the group directly. You must first remove all members and then leave the group yourself.

Steps to delete a group:

  1. Open the group and tap the group name to see the member list.
  2. Remove each member one by one.
  3. After removing everyone, tap Exit Group.
  4. After you leave, a Delete Group option will appear. Tap it to confirm.

This action cannot be undone, so use it carefully. If you are a regular member, not an admin, you can only leave the group. You cannot delete it for everyone.

How to Create a WhatsApp Group Invite Link

An invite link makes it easy to add new members without saving their numbers one by one.

On Mobile:

  1. Open the group and tap the group name.
  2. Tap Invite to Group via Link.
  3. The link will appear. You can send it directly, copy it, share it, or create a QR code.

On Computer:

  1. Open WhatsApp Web or the WhatsApp desktop app.
  2. Open the group and click Group Info.
  3. Click Invite to Group via Link, then copy or send the link.

Only group admins can create or share this link.

How to Reset a WhatsApp Group Invite Link

If you want to stop new people from joining with an old link, you can create a new one.

  1. Open the group and tap the group name.
  2. Tap Invite to Group via Link.
  3. Select Reset Link.

The old link will stop working right away. A new link will be created, which you can share with others.
