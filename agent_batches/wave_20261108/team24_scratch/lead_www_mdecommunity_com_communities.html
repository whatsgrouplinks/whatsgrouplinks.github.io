We’ve Got Answers

Here is what most people ask before joining

What is MDE Community?

MDE Community is a curated network of WhatsApp groups, social events, and collaborations designed to help digital nomads, expats, and locals in Medellín connect quickly and authentically.

Who is it for?

Our community is built for globally-minded people—remote workers, creatives, entrepreneurs, and locals—who value real friendships and meaningful experiences.

How do I join?

Simply visit our Communities page, browse the interest categories, and click “Join” to access the WhatsApp group that fits you best. You can join multiple groups based on your interests.

Is there a membership fee?

Joining is free. However, MDE Community runs on member support through donations via Ko-Fi, which help us organize events, manage groups, and keep everything running smoothly
