Guidelines to follow in the WhatsApp groups:

  1. It is important to be professional and polite to all the members of the group. Do not discriminate, harass, or use foul language in your interactions with other people. 
  2. Do not argue, especially when it is negative. It is normal for people to have different opinions; thus, it is essential to solve conflicts politely. 
  3. Make sure that all posts are related to the group's purpose, which is to share information on issues concerning pursuing an MS in the USA. 
  4. Avoid posting anything that is not related to the topic, advertising, or anything that may be considered spam. 
  5. Do not share your or other people’s personal/ sensitive information, such as house address, bank details, or identification numbers on the group.
  6. People should not repost any pictures or content from the group without the consent of the people involved. 
  7. Avoid posting too many messages on the group if the messages are not likely to be useful to other members. 
  8. This is the right place to ask your doubts about admission, scholarships, visas, and accommodation if you have any.
  9. The following are prohibited: forwarding of chain messages and other unverified information. Make sure all the information that is shared is correct and appropriate. 
  10. The group is receptive to enhancements as well as ideas from other members. If one has any suggestions or complaints, kindly report them to the admin.

    2. Can I join these groups from my USA number?

Yes, you can join these WhatsApp groups using your USA number. With the help of WhatsApp, people can add phone numbers to groups irrespective of the country code. You can join by clicking on the links given above in the blog.

    3. Can I exit from these groups whenever I want?

These WhatsApp groups are always open for your participation, and you have full control of any group you wish to join. You also have the option to opt out of the group if you think that it is no longer useful to you or if it is getting too much. You are not obligated to give any explanations for leaving the group.

    4. Can I get my college updates in these groups?

Yes, you can get college updates in these WhatsApp groups. These may be on admission, scholarship, course, events, visa procedure, accommodation, job vacancies, examination, and alumni meetings, among others. To receive these updates, it is necessary to join the groups, be active, change the settings of notifications, and follow the rules of the groups. This way, you will be updated with important college information, which will help you in your college education.
