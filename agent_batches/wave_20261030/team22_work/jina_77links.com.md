### FAQ-Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. **What are WhatsApp group links?**

WhatsApp group links (also called WhatsApp invite links) are unique URLs that allow anyone to join a specific WhatsApp group without needing to be manually added by an admin. When you click a WhatsApp group link, you are taken directly to WhatsApp where you can see the group name and choose to join with a single tap.

### 2. **Are WhatsApp group links safe to click?**

WhatsApp group links published on 77Links are manually verified and checked for safety before going live. However, as a general rule, always check the group name and member count before joining any group. If something looks suspicious, trust your instincts and do not join.

### 3.**How many members can a WhatsApp group have?**

As of 2026, WhatsApp allows up to 1,024 members in a single group. If a group is full, the invite link will show an error. In that case, look for another group in the same category on 77Links.

### 4. **Can I submit my own WhatsApp group link to 77Links?**

Yes! Group admins can submit their WhatsApp group links for free using our Submit Group form. After a quick review, your group will be listed on 77Links and start receiving new members.

### 5. **How do I find active WhatsApp groups for a specific topic?**

Use the search bar at the top of 77Links and type your topic — for example “cricket”, “jobs in Pakistan”, or “IELTS preparation”. You will instantly see all matching WhatsApp groups with active invite links. You can also browse categories in the main menu.

### 6. **How do I create a WhatsApp group link?**

Open WhatsApp and go to your group. Tap the group name at the top to open Group Info. Scroll down to find “Invite via Link” and tap it. You can copy the link, share it directly, or create a QR code. Share your link on 77Links to attract new members.

### 7. **What happens if a WhatsApp group link expires?**

WhatsApp group links can expire if the admin resets them or if the group reaches its member limit. On 77Links, we monitor links regularly and replace expired ones. If you find a broken link, please report it using the report button and we will remove or update it within 24 hours.

### 8. Can I recover deleted WhatsApp messages from a group chat?

If you’ve backed up your WhatsApp data using Google Drive or iCloud, you can restore deleted messages by reinstalling the app and restoring from your latest backup.

### 9. How do I block someone in a WhatsApp group?

You cannot block someone specifically within a group, but you can block their contact directly from their profile. Once blocked, they won’t be able to message you individually, but you will still see their messages in the group.

### 10. How can I report spam in WhatsApp group?

To report spam in a group, open the group, tap on the group info, and scroll to “Report.” You can also report individual messages as spam by long-pressing the message and selecting “Report.”

### 11. What should I do if I suspect a WhatsApp group link is a scam?

If you suspect a group link is a scam, avoid clicking on it and report the link or sender to WhatsApp. Scams can lead to data theft, malware, or unwanted access to your personal information.

### 12. Can I mute notifications for a WhatsApp group?

Yes, you can mute notifications for a group by opening the group chat, tapping on the group name, and selecting “Mute Notifications.” You can choose to mute it for 8 hours, 1 week, or 1 year.

### 13. How can I manage privacy settings in WhatsApp groups?

To manage your privacy settings in groups, go to “Settings,” select “Privacy,” and adjust who can see your profile photo, last seen, status, and who can add you to groups.

### 14. Can I remove someone from a WhatsApp group?

Only group admins can remove members from a group. If you are an admin, go to the group info, find the member you want to remove, tap on their name, and select “Remove.”

### 15. How many participants can a WhatsApp group have?

As of the latest update, these groups can hold up to 1024 participants, allowing for larger communities and discussions.
