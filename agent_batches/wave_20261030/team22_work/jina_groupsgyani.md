## Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about **Whatsapp** groups.

What is a WhatsApp group?

A WhatsApp group is a chat community where members message each other around a shared interest. Anyone with the invite link can join and take part. Groupsgyani lists public WhatsApp groups by category with verified invite links you can join for free.

How do I join a WhatsApp group?

Browse the groups on this page or search by topic, open a listing, tap the join button, and accept the disclaimer. You'll be redirected to the WhatsApp invite, which opens directly in WhatsApp if installed or in your browser otherwise. Joining is free and needs no Groupsgyani account.

How many WhatsApp groups are on Groupsgyani?

Groupsgyani lists 1,235 active WhatsApp groups across every category, last updated October 2026. Inactive groups are swept twice a week, so the count reflects links reachable at the last automated check.

Are WhatsApp group links on Groupsgyani free?

Yes — every WhatsApp group link on Groupsgyani is free to join. We list user-submitted invite links, validate them before they go live, and never charge to join a group.

Are these WhatsApp group links safe and active?

Groupsgyani validates each invite link before publishing and re-checks listings on a rolling schedule, removing dead links twice a week. The groups themselves are user-run, so stay alert inside any chat and never share OTPs, banking details, or passwords.
