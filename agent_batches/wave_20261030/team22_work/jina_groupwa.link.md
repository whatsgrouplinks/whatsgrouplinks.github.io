## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a WhatsApp group link?

It is an invite URL (usually starting with chat.whatsapp.com) that opens a preview of a WhatsApp group. Tap Join Group, and you become a member instantly — no approval needed.

### Is it free to join WhatsApp groups?

Yes. Joining any WhatsApp group is completely free. If a group asks you to pay, leave it and report it — genuine groups never charge entry fees.

### How many members can a WhatsApp group have?

WhatsApp allows up to 1,024 members per group. If a group is full, try a similar one from the same category on groupwa.link.

### Can I leave a group without anyone knowing?

Yes. When you leave, only the group admins are notified — other members don’t get an alert.

### How do I create my own group invite link?

Open your group, tap its name, then tap Invite via Link. Copy the link and submit it for free at groupwa.link/add-group/ to get more members.

### Are the groups on groupwa.link safe?

We review every submission before listing, but groups are run by their own admins. Never share passwords, bank details or OTPs, and report abusive groups to WhatsApp.
