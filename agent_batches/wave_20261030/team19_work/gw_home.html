What Is a WhatsApp Group Link?

A WhatsApp Group Link is a special invite URL that takes you straight to a WhatsApp group. Tap it, see a preview of the group — its name, icon, and member count — and hit Join Group. No admin approval needed, no phone numbers exchanged. It is the fastest way to walk into any public community on WhatsApp.

Every link on groupwa.link is submitted by users and reviewed before listing, so you spend less time hunting and more time chatting.

How to Join a WhatsApp Group in 3 Steps

  3. Tap “Join Group” — you are in. Read the pinned rules and say hello.

How GroupWa.Link Works

  1. Someone shares a group — a group admin pastes their WhatsApp invite link on our Add WhatsApp Group page, picks the right category, and sends it in. Free, no account needed.
