About Us

We started this directory after getting tired of clicking WhatsApp invite links that led nowhere — dead pages, expired invites, groups at full capacity. The directories we found were not maintaining their listings.

So we built a directory with a simple promise: every group is reviewed before it goes live. We check that the invite link actually opens, that the name and category make sense, and that the submission looks genuine. After publishing, inactive links are flagged and removed.

We only look at what the submitter provides — name, image, description. We never enter groups or read any messages. What goes on inside a community is between its members.

Our mission is to make it effortless to find your people on WhatsApp — study partners, job alerts, hobby communities, local groups — whatever you are looking for.
