Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a WhatsApp group from this site?

Find a group you like, open its page, and tap the green "Join on WhatsApp" button. WhatsApp will open directly at the group's join screen. No account or sign-up on this site is needed.

Are the group invite links checked?

Yes. Every submitted group is reviewed before publishing — we verify the invite link opens, the name and category make sense, and the submission looks genuine. Inactive links are flagged and removed.

Why does a link say it is expired or revoked?

WhatsApp lets group admins reset their invite link at any time, which deactivates the old one. Groups also stop accepting members at 1,024 participants. If you spot a dead link, please report it on the group page.

How do I add my own WhatsApp group?

Go to the Submit page, paste your WhatsApp invite link, choose a category and country, and send it in. Listings are reviewed and usually go live within 24 hours. It is completely free.

Is this directory free to use?

Yes — browsing, searching, joining, and submitting groups are all free. There is no account, no paid plan, and no catch.

Do you read messages inside the groups?

No. We only check the invite link, name, image, and description provided at submission time. What happens inside a group is between you and that community.
