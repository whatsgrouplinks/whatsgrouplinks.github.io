Best Study WhatsApp Groups 2026: CSS, MDCAT, UPSC, IELTS & More

Find the best study WhatsApp groups for 2026 — CSS, MDCAT, UPSC, IELTS, board exams and more. Free notes, past papers, and daily quizzes shared by toppers.

Study WhatsApp groups are the quiet backbone of exam preparation across Pakistan and India in 2026. Aspirants share PDF notes, past papers, solved MCQs, and daily quizzes — often within minutes of them being released.

CSS & PMS groups focus on current affairs, essay outlines, and past-paper discussions. The best ones run daily current-affairs quizzes and weekly essay topics with peer review.

MDCAT & ECAT groups are goldmines for chapter-wise MCQs, NUMS past papers, and video lecture links. Many are run by recent toppers who post their own handwritten notes for free.

UPSC & SSC groups share NCERT summaries, The Hindu editorials, and monthly current-affairs compilations. Look for groups that pin a syllabus tracker so you always know what is covered.

IELTS & English-learning groups run daily speaking practice, writing task corrections, and vocabulary drops. The most active ones pair members as speaking partners.

How to pick a good study group: check that admins post regularly, that files are organized by topic, and that spam is deleted quickly. Leave groups that are 90% forwards and 10% study.
