How to Join WhatsApp Groups in 2026: Step-by-Step Guide

Learn how to join any WhatsApp group in 2026 — via invite link, QR code, or admin approval. Full step-by-step guide for Android, iPhone, and WhatsApp Web.

Joining a WhatsApp group in 2026 takes less than a minute — if you know where to tap. Most groups are joined through an invite link that looks like chat.whatsapp.com/XXXX. When you tap it, WhatsApp opens directly on the group’s preview screen with the group name, photo, and member count, plus a green Join Group button.

On Android: tap the invite link, review the group preview, then tap Join Group. You are in instantly — no approval needed for public invite links. On iPhone: the flow is identical — tap the link, tap Join Group in the preview. On WhatsApp Web: click the link in your browser, scan the QR if asked, then click Join Group in the web preview.

Some groups require admin approval. In that case you will see a Request to Join button instead. Your request sits in a queue until an admin approves it — this can take minutes or days depending on the group.

Before joining, always check three things: the group name and photo (does it match what you expected?), the member count (groups cap at 1,024 members — a full group will reject you), and the description (legitimate groups explain their purpose and rules).

If a link says “expired” or “revoked”, the admin reset the invite link. Old links die the moment a new one is generated. Your only fix is to get the fresh link from the admin or from an updated directory listing.

Safety matters. Never share passwords, bank details, or one-time codes in any group — admins will never ask for them. Be cautious of groups promising free money or prizes; those are classic scam setups.
